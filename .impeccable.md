## Design Context

### Users

Novel Vibe serves Chinese web-novel writers who spend long, focused sessions planning, drafting, revising, and managing a growing manuscript with an AI editorial team. The interface should support sustained desktop writing first while remaining complete and composed on mobile.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, literary, and meticulously crafted. The product should feel like a room made for writing in silence: calm enough to disappear during deep work, precise enough to inspire confidence, and distinctive enough to feel authored rather than assembled from generic SaaS components.

### Aesthetic Direction

Create a contemporary editorial room rather than a nostalgic study, an ornamental Chinese-style interface, or a conventional productivity dashboard. Use warm, tactile paper; ink-tinted neutrals; disciplined typography; measured asymmetry; and finely resolved details. Motion is secondary to composition and material quality. When present, it should express spatial continuity, state changes, and the settling of paper or tools—never spectacle.

The interface supports light and dark themes. Both should preserve the same quiet editorial character. Avoid neon-on-dark AI aesthetics, decorative gradients, generic rounded cards, excessive glass effects, ornamental pseudo-tradition, and motion that competes with reading or writing.

### Design Principles

1. **The manuscript is the quiet center.** Surrounding tools should frame the work, not compete with it.
2. **Luxury comes from proportion and restraint.** Earn depth through typography, spacing, edges, and material contrast before adding decoration.
3. **One room, not many templates.** Every surface should feel part of the same editorial environment while retaining a clear functional hierarchy.
4. **Motion explains place and state.** Favor orchestrated entrances, continuity, and precise feedback; keep idle surfaces still and honor reduced-motion preferences.
5. **Dense tools, calm composition.** Preserve professional capability and information density without turning the interface into a generic dashboard.
